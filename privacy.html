Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. What Digital Dokan is

Digital Dokan is point-of-sale (POS), customer Khata (credit ledger) and inventory software for small shops. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to the information you enter into the software.

2. Where your shop data is stored today

Your shop data is stored on your own device. The current version of the app saves your products, customers, sales, Khata records, expenses and settings in your browser's local storage on the computer, tablet or phone you use. It is not uploaded to our servers.

  • If you clear your browser data, or use a different device or browser, your saved data will not be there.
  • Please use the app's Download Backup feature regularly and keep the backup file somewhere safe.
  • Anyone who uses the same browser on a shared device may be able to see the data saved in that browser. Please sign out and protect shared devices.

We are building secure cloud accounts so that every shop's data is stored under that shop's own private account. We will update this policy before that change goes live.

3. Information you share with us directly

When you contact us or request a demo, we receive the details you choose to send us, for example your name, shop name, city, phone/WhatsApp number and email address. We use these only to respond to you, set up your account, provide support and manage your subscription.

4. Payments

Subscriptions are currently paid manually by EasyPaisa or JazzCash. When you send us a payment receipt (for example a screenshot), we use it only to verify your payment and activate your plan. We do not receive or store your mobile wallet password or PIN — never share those with us or anyone else.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information or your shop data.
  • We do not use your customers' details for advertising.
  • We never ask for your account password by WhatsApp, email or phone.

6. Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form on this website are delivered to us through our form provider so that we can read and reply to them.

7. Your choices

  • You can export a backup of your shop data at any time from the app's Settings.
  • You can delete all locally saved app data at any time from the app's Settings (Danger Zone). Please download a backup first if you may need the data again.
  • To ask what contact details we hold about you, or to have them removed, contact us using the details below.
