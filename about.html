About Digital Dokan

Software that respects how real shops work

Digital Dokan is a POS and Udhaar Khata app for small retail shops. It replaces the paper register with one calm workspace for billing, customer credit, stock and profit — without asking shopkeepers to change how they run their day.

Our story

It started with a paper register

Every small shop knows the same scene. Sales go into one notebook, udhaar into another, and the real state of the business lives in the owner memory. It works — until a page is lost, a balance is argued over, or a busy day leaves no time to write anything down.

Digital Dokan was built to keep the good parts of that system — trust, speed, personal credit — and remove the fragile parts. The khata is still a khata. It is just written clearly, kept safe, and always one search away.

Forgotten udhaar

Balances that live in memory get disputed. Digital Dokan keeps every customer ledger with dates, payments and a running balance.

Stock surprises

Empty shelves are usually discovered by a customer. Low-stock alerts and expiry dates warn the shop first.

Guessed profit

A full galla does not always mean a good day. Galla day-close and Munafa reports show what the day really earned.

What it does

One app for the whole shop day

Fast billing

POS billing with barcode scanning, held bills, discounts and easy returns — with receipts on a thermal printer or straight to WhatsApp.

Khata & wasooli

Customer ledgers with promise dates and credit limits, WhatsApp reminders, and a Mera Khata link that customers can open themselves.

Stock that watches itself

Inventory with low-stock alerts, expiry and batch tracking, ready starter catalogues and CSV import when a shop moves from paper.

Money clarity

Galla day-close, Munafa profit worked out from real costs, plus expenses and supplier purchases — all in one place.
