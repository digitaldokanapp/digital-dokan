FAQ

Questions owners ask about price

How much does Digital Dokan cost after the trial?

Pro is Rs. 2,500 per month, or Rs. 22,500 per year — the yearly plan saves you Rs. 7,500. One plan includes every feature; there are no per-feature or per-user charges.

What happens when my 7-day trial ends?

Your data is kept safe — nothing is deleted. The app simply pauses new entries until you choose a plan. Pick a plan, pay by mobile wallet, and you continue exactly where you left off.

How do I pay? I do not have a bank card.

No card is needed. Message us on WhatsApp (0325 7633875), pay by EasyPaisa or NayaPay to 03257633875 (Faiza Aqeel) or JazzCash to 03004428110 (Aqeel Yousaf), and send the receipt in the same chat. Your plan is activated after a quick verification.

Can I switch from monthly to yearly later?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp whenever you want to switch, and we will move your shop to the yearly plan — your data, items and history stay exactly as they are.

Is there a setup fee or a charge per bill?

No. There is no setup fee, no per-bill charge and no charge per staff login. The monthly or yearly price is the whole price.
