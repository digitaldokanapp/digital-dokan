Where the others stop

Good tools — with a missing half

Khata apps stop at the ledger

Udhaar Book and KhataBook record udhaar well — but behind them there is no real shop counter: no barcode billing, no thermal receipts, no stock that watches itself. Digital Dokan runs the counter and the khata in one place, so a sale and its udhaar are never two separate jobs.

Loyverse has no khata at all

A polished free POS from overseas — but no udhaar ledger, no WhatsApp bills, and the extras a growing shop needs (staff roles, advanced stock) are paid add-ons billed per store in dollars. Digital Dokan includes them in one flat price.

Vyapar is built for another country tax

Vyapar is shaped around Indian GST billing, and it has no iPhone app. If your shop does not file GST, you are carrying software made for someone else rules. Digital Dokan is shaped for everyday retail — billing, khata and stock, nothing you do not need.

SYEZPOS charges more for the same idea

A local POS with a khata at Rs 3,000 a month. Digital Dokan gives you the complete system — billing, khata, stock, expiry, reports and staff logins — at Rs 2,500 a month, with every feature included in the one price.
