What It Is

From the first bill of the day to the last entry in the khata

Most shops run on three separate things: a billing habit, a paper khata and memory. Digital Dokan joins all three into one flow — sell at the POS and the stock drops, the unpaid part lands in the customer's khata, and the day's galla and munafa build themselves. Nothing is typed twice, and nothing depends on one person's memory.

Why Shops Switch

The three leaks every register hides

▣

Forgotten udhaar

Balances written in a register are balances nobody chases. Digital Dokan lists exactly who owes what, and who promised to pay today.

◉

Empty shelves

Fast items finish silently between stock counts. Low-stock alerts reach you while there is still time to reorder.

▦

Core Modules

What is inside Digital Dokan

POS Billing

Bills in seconds, even at rush hour

  • Barcode scanning with a USB scanner or phone camera
  • Hold a bill and recall it when the customer returns
  • Discounts in percent or rupees on any bill
  • Split payments — cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa and part-udhaar on one bill
  • Sales returns against the original bill, with stock restored

Udhaar Khata & Wasooli

Credit that collects itself

  • A clear ledger for every customer: sales, payments, balance
  • Promise dates and credit limits on each customer
  • A daily wasooli list — who promised to pay today, who is overdue
  • One-tap WhatsApp statements and polite payment reminders

Stock & Inventory

Know what you have, before it runs out

  • Live stock levels that update with every sale and purchase
  • Your own low-stock level per item, with alerts before it finishes
  • One-tap WhatsApp reorder message to the item's supplier
  • Ready starter catalogues for kiryana, pharmacy, mobile, garments and bakery shops, plus CSV import

Barcode & Labels

For items with — and without — barcodes

  • A barcode field on every product
  • Scan to bill and scan to stock-in
  • Built-in Code39 label printing for loose or unlabelled items

Expiry & Batch Tracking

Made for pharmacies and food shops

  • Batch number and expiry date recorded at stock-in
  • Near-expiry lists so stock sells before it spoils
  • The POS warns you before an expired item can be billed

Galla & Munafa Reports

Close the day with numbers that match

  • Daily galla: opening cash, cash in and out, expected vs counted
  • Real munafa (profit) after purchase costs and daily expenses
  • Sales reports for today, this week and this month
  • Best-selling and dead-stock items at a glance

Staff Logins & Roles

Your team, with the right access

  • Separate logins for owner, manager and cashier
  • Cashiers cannot delete records, change settings or manage staff
  • Corrections like cancelling a bill need an owner or manager
  • Every shop's data stays locked to that shop alone

Mera Khata — Customer Khata Links

Your customer checks their own balance

  • A private link for every customer showing only their own khata
  • No app or login needed on the customer's side
  • Fewer 'show me my hisaab' disputes at the counter

FAQ

Questions owners ask about the features

Is every feature included in one plan, or do I pay per feature?

Every feature is included in the single Pro plan — billing, khata, stock, reports, staff logins and everything else on this page. There are no per-feature charges and no add-ons to unlock later.

How many items can I add during the free trial?

The 7-day free trial lets you run your shop with up to 50 items, with every feature open. When you move to Pro, your items, customers and history carry over exactly as they are.

Does Digital Dokan work without internet?

Yes. Billing, khata and stock keep working on your device when the internet drops, and everything syncs to the cloud when the connection returns. A counter never has to stop for a weak signal.

Can my cashier use the app without seeing my profit?

Yes. Staff get their own logins with roles. A cashier can bill and take payments, but cannot delete records, change settings, manage staff or see subscription details. Corrections such as cancelling a bill need an owner or a manager.

Which printers and scanners does it support?

Digital Dokan prints receipts on 58mm and 80mm thermal printers and full A4 invoices, and bills with a USB barcode scanner or your phone camera. It can also print barcode labels for items that come without one.
